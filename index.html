About Onimeku

Onimeku is an Android app for discovering and watching anime. Browse a large catalogue, follow the seasonal release schedule, keep your own list, and pick up right where you left off — every episode in our library comes with Indonesian and English subtitles.

Features

  • Anime catalogue with posters, synopses, and episode lists
  • Indonesian & English subtitles on every episode
  • Release schedule so you never miss a new episode
  • Watch history and progress synced to your account
  • Personal list and offline downloads
  • Sign in with email, Google, or Discord
