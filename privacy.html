Privacy Policy

Onimeku · Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information the Onimeku Android app ("Onimeku", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account or sign in, we store your email address and a user identifier. If you sign in with Google or Discord, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from that provider.
  • Activity data. To sync across the app we store your watch history and playback progress, your personal list (bookmarks), and app preferences linked to your account.
  • On-device data. Downloaded episodes, subtitle files, and settings are stored locally on your device and are removed when you delete them or uninstall the app.

We do not collect your contacts, messages, precise location, or files outside the app's own storage.

2. How we use information

  • To provide sign-in and keep your account secure.
  • To sync your watch history, progress, and list to your account.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Service providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run the app: Supabase (account authentication and database hosting), Google and Discord (only if you choose social sign-in), and AniList (anime metadata and release schedules). These providers process data only as needed to provide their part of the service.

4. Data retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from within the app (Profile → Delete Account); this permanently deletes your account and its associated data from our systems. On-device data is deleted when you remove downloads or uninstall the app.

5. Security

Account traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and passwords are handled by our authentication provider using industry-standard hashing. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

6. Children

Onimeku is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with its effective date shown at the top.
